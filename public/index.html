Offline brain

Your system in one file

        
Nyx captures your apps and settings into a tiny signed file — not a disk image. Drop it on a fresh Windows and it rebuilds everything, asking what to keep.

Every action is statically audited before it runs. Read-only tasks auto-run; anything risky needs your OK; destructive commands are blocked cold.

      

    

  


  

    

      
How it works

      
Ask in plain words. Nyx does the rest.

    

    

      
You ask
"Install Steam" or "back up my setup" — in any language, your words.

      
It reasons
One on-device pass decides: answer, act, or snapshot — and writes the exact command.

      
It's audited
The command passes a zero-trust validator before anything touches your system.

      
It's done
Safe steps run instantly; risky ones wait for one tap. You stay in control.
